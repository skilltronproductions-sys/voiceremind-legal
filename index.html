Voice Remind — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Voice Remind is made by Skilltronics. This page explains what information Voice Remind collects, why, and what happens to it.

What Voice Remind stores on your device

Your reminders — the message, and if you gave one, a place name and the location you saved for it — are stored locally on your phone. We don't keep a copy of this on a server tied to your identity.

What leaves your device, and why

  • Creating a reminder: the text of what you type or say is sent once to an AI service (xAI's Grok) to turn it into a reminder. This is the only network call in the normal, everyday use of the app.
  • Finding a place by address: if you type an address instead of using your current location, that address is sent to OpenStreetMap's free Nominatim service to look up its coordinates. No account or identifying information is sent with it.
  • Location data: Voice Remind uses your phone's location service to detect when you arrive at or leave a place you've set a reminder for. This runs on your device through your phone's own operating system — it is not transmitted to us or anyone else.
  • Google Tasks (optional): if you choose to connect Google Tasks in Settings, your plain (non-location) reminders are also sent to your own Google Tasks list. This only happens if you turn it on, and only for reminders you create after turning it on.
  • AI assistant connector (optional): if you choose to connect Voice Remind to an AI chat assistant like Claude or Grok, a reminder created that way is sent to our server so it can reach your phone and get scheduled. This only happens for reminders created through that connection, and only if you've set it up.

What we don't do

Voice Remind has no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Your choices

You can delete any reminder at any time, which removes it from your device. You can disconnect Google Tasks or the AI assistant connector at any time in Settings.
